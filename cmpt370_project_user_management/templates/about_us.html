Hello!

Thank you for visiting our meal planner website. We are a group of CMPT 370 student who were inspired by our peers talking about how difficult it is to manage meal prep between classes. From those conversations we decided to develop a website that will make meal planning, grocery shopping, and finding recipe's easier. Knowing most people live with friends or family we wanted to make sure all meal plans were collaborative. Anyone can add, deleted, comment, on and react to the recipes.
